Travel Packing Tips

Packing well can make a trip much easier and less stressful. Planning ahead and bringing only what you actually need can help save space, keep your luggage organized, and make traveling more comfortable.

Travel Packing Checklist

  • Passport or ID
  • Phone and charger
  • Comfortable clothes and shoes
  • Toiletries and personal items
  • Travel documents and reservations
  • Medications and other essentials
  • Headphones or entertainment for the trip

Plan Outfits Ahead of Time

Planning outfits before a trip can help prevent overpacking. I think it is helpful to choose clothing that can be worn in different combinations and to check the weather before deciding what to bring.

Stay Organized

Keeping similar items together makes it easier to find everything while traveling. Packing cubes or separate bags can be useful for organizing clothes, toiletries, shoes, and smaller items.

Keep Important Items With You

Important items like identification, travel documents, medications, chargers, and valuables should be easy to access. Keeping essentials in a personal item or carry-on can also be helpful in case checked luggage is delayed.

Taking a little extra time to organize before leaving can make the entire travel experience easier and leave more time to enjoy the trip.
